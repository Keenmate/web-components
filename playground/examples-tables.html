TB02 · Reference table

.reference-table — the API / option table most example pages use: full width, every cell boxed; the names in its first column never break mid-word. tr.is-highlight tints a row with the accent. On a phone every row becomes a card — the name its title, each other cell under its column's header.

TB07 · Stacking on a phone, or not

Below 768 px a .reference-table stacks its rows into cards; the kit labels each cell with its column's header (data-label, unless the cell has one). A grid read down its columns keeps the sideways scroll with .table-scroll; any other table stacks with .table-stack. Narrow the window to see both.
