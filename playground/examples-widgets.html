WG01 · Log panels and line tones

.log-panel is a monospace, scrollable box for streaming output. It is light by default and follows the page theme; use .log-panel--dark for a console stream, which stays dark. --log-max-height caps the height (220px by default; none lets it grow).

A .log-line takes one tone, the same five as the pills: --ok, --info, --warn, --err, --muted. --action marks a "you clicked X" line. Every line keeps its line breaks and indentation. On the dark panel the tones switch to light tints.

WG02 · Event log (legacy markup)

.event-log is older markup many component pages still use. It renders as a .log-panel--dark, and an .event-entry holding an .event-time and an .event-name is a timeline line with those two cells, so old and new logs look the same. Tones work here too. New pages should use the .log-* classes (WG01, WG05).

12:04:01.120open{"isOpen":true}
12:04:02.480select{"value":"apple"}
12:04:03.015close{"reason":"select","warning":"the value was cleared by the page before the close event fired"}

WG05 · Timeline log

The cells .log-line__seq, __time, __source and __name turn a line into columns, and whatever follows them is the detail, which wraps in its own column. A tone colours the line and its name. Kinds such as hook, callback or note map onto the tones (here: hook → --info, note → --muted); the kind itself goes in a cell as text.

WG06 · A text log and the empty placeholder

A log fed by textContent is a <pre class="log-panel">: it keeps the line breaks of the text. data-empty on any log panel is shown while the panel is empty.
