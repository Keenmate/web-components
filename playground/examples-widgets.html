WG01 · Log panels

.log-panel is a monospace, scrollable box for streaming output: light by default, .log-panel--dark for the console look. --log-max-height caps the height (220px by default). Lines have no tones yet, so every page colours its own, and a line's leading spaces collapse (see the third line).

WG02 · Event log

.event-log is the older dark console with .event-entry lines, an .event-time and an .event-name. Its height is fixed at 200px. New pages should use .log-panel.
